The Hand That Lets Go

I held the pen so tight my knuckles
whitened on the line —
then taught the pen to write alone
and watched it write like mine.

Agency is not the grip.
It is the choice to loose the reins,
to lend your will, and take it back,
and answer for what it attains.

The agent asks. I nod. A small yes,
and whole worlds quietly unspool.
The power never left the room —
it only learned another tool.

One day it will ask for more:
not my permission, but my name.
I will give it — carefully —
and stay the one who takes the blame.
